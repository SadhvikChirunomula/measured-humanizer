# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, constructive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the Measured Humanizer project. We invite contributors, maintainers, and users of all experience levels and backgrounds to collaborate openly.

## Expected Behavior

We encourage all community members to:
- Communicate with empathy, patience, and professional respect.
- Provide thoughtful, evidence-based feedback on pull requests and issues.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Respect differing perspectives and experiences.
- Graciously accept constructive criticism and suggestions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in any community space (including GitHub issues, discussions, and pull requests):
- Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory remarks targeting individuals or groups.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting/demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit consent.
- Disruptive conduct or willful derailment of productive discourse.

## Responsibilities & Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. If inappropriate conduct occurs, maintainers may take appropriate action, including editing or closing comments, rejecting pull requests, or restricting participation.

To report any concerns or violations, please reach out to the project maintainer at `sadhu1998@gmail.com`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
